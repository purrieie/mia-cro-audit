04

How the estimates were worked out

R35
Growth model figuresTrust ₹37.2 L, offer rules ₹43.9 L; together ₹77.0 L after overlap (₹81.1 L before)
Troopod's working
How to check
Troopod's growth model on 45 days of Mia's website data, with a 40% margin assumption. The most each could add in 12 months, not a forecast: making trust visible ₹37.2 L, clear offer rules ₹43.9 L; ₹81.1 L added up, ₹77.0 L after removing the overlap between them.
Date range
2 Sep to 16 Oct 2026
R36
Revenue estimates in the plan₹71 L, ₹27 L, ₹83 L, ₹1.0 L per 1,000 coin-page visitors
Troopod's working
How to check
₹71 L: 16,649 bag viewers × 1% × 28.9% (checkout to order, 884 ÷ 3,061) × ₹11,379 (average order) × 13 four-week periods. ₹27 L: 1,829 shoppers at payment × 1% × ₹11,379 × 13. ₹83 L: 5,642 store requests × 1% × ₹11,379 × 13. Coins: 1.31 per 100 views × 1,000 × ₹8,008. Revenue, not profit; store order values may differ.
Date range
18 Sep to 15 Oct 2026
