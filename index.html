Who shops Mia's website

Five kinds of shopper, built from what people add to bag, search for, click and buy (Mia's website, four weeks to 15 October 2026). Each needs something different from the bag. The plan is built around them.

The diamond shortlister

13,992diamond pieces added to bag, more than any other type; 1.1% boughtR17
What we see
Mostly on a phone. On phones only 6.0% of diamond pieces added to bag reach checkout, against 22.5% on desktop.R17 5,642 people asked a store to call them about a piece in four weeks, six for every online buyer (section 8).
What they want
Certainty about a ₹20K to ₹80K piece: real stones, a certificate, a way back, and often to see it in a store first.
What the bag should give
Certificate and returns by the button, EMI on the stone value, and "available at stores near you".

The festival coin buyer

53%of gold coins added to bag reach checkout; 12.6% are boughtR17
What we see
The best converters in the bag. Coins are 56% of pieces bought after landing on the homepage (92 of 165).R20 57,249 visits land on "Gold rate today"; 3 buy (section 7). The 0.5 g coin is 958 of about 1,565 coins bought in 90 days.R18
What they want
A known weight at today's rate, delivered before Dhanteras, with purity they can trust.
What the site should give
Today's rate with 0.5 g, 1 g and 2 g coins on the gold rate page; purity and delivery date on the coin line.

The silver and small-ticket buyer

10,727silver pieces added to bag; 5.1% boughtR17
What we see
Average piece ₹1,800 to ₹3,400. "Silver ring", "Payal" and "Anklet" are top site searches.
What they want
A quick, low-risk purchase. At ₹1,800, ₹500 off is a quarter of the price, so a refused MIA500 can end the sale.
What the bag should give
Guest checkout and the code applied when they qualify.

The gifter

60%of the visitors Google can identify are menR21
What we see
"Under ₹15K" is the most clicked price tile.R22 3,333 people had a birthday or anniversary code refused, most likely shopping for someone else's occasion.R23
What they want
A present that arrives on time, looks like a present, and can be exchanged.
What the bag should give
Mia's gift wrap, note and sticker, which are built and switched off until the warehouse can fulfil them.

The returning member

16%of website orders redeem loyalty pointsR24
What we see
4,608 people opened the bag already logged in. Returning visitors on desktop who start on the homepage convert at 1.18%, the highest of any group.R25
What they want
To buy again quickly and use what they have earned.
What checkout should give
The saved address selected, the points balance shown, one tap to pay, and the purchase plan online (section 9).
Product types are grouped from product names, and counts are pieces, not people. Google can tell age and gender for about 27% of visitors.
