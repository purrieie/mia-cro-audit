Next cycle · bag, checkout, payment and homepage

Mia's bag is five different shoppers, and it is built for none of them

This version starts from the people in the bag: what they came for, what the page gives them today, and what it should give them. It uses Mia's GA4 website stream (28 days to 15 October 2026), Mia's live pages, the Troopod team's logged-in walkthrough, and a teardown of six competitors' bags captured on 16 October 2026. Every claim is labelled OBSERVED seen on a page or in data, INFERRED our reading of it, or MISSING not yet known.

01

The one-page summary

For the ecommerce manager: the three biggest leaks, the five actions that matter most, and the decisions only Mia can make.

64%
Leak 1: the login wall
9,651 of 15,074 logged-out bag viewers never open the login. Mia's sister brand Tanishq already offers "Guest Checkout" at the same step.
52%
Leak 2: the payment page
1,829 went to payment, 884 paid. A ₹1.82 lakh order was offered Cards and Netbanking only. 402 failures carry no reason.
6%
Leak 3: diamonds on mobile
Of 10,285 diamond pieces added to bag on mobile, 612 reached checkout. On desktop it is 22.5%. Diamond is the most-added product type.
Five actions, in order
  1. Open a guest path at the bag, phone number only, as Tanishq does.
  2. Show only coupons the shopper can use, and say why one is refused.
  3. Fix payment: confirm methods by order value, UPI to the jewellery limit, a summary on the payment page.
  4. Bring the store into the bag for diamond pieces: stock near you, reserve to try.
  5. Fix the tracking that hides pre-book orders, coupon reasons and payment failures.
Decisions only Mia can make
  1. Leadership: guest checkout and what identity is required; whether a store sale started online counts as an online win.
  2. Offers: the eligibility rules for MIA500, MIBD12, MIAD15, and whether to auto-apply.
  3. Payments: UPI category limit, EMI on stone value, with Juspay.
  4. Operations: store stock feed for the bag; gift wrap and message.
  5. Analytics: are pre-book orders recorded as purchases?

Who is in Mia's bag: five shoppers

Built from what people add, search, click and buy on the website. Unit counts are items added to bag over 28 days. Each card says what that shopper came for, what the bag gives them today, and what it should give them.

1. The diamond shortlister

13,992diamond pieces added to bag, the largest type; 1.1% bought
Who
OBSERVEDMostly mobile. Mobile adds reach checkout 6.0% of the time, desktop 22.5%. 12,411 mobile users opened "Find in store". 89,632 sessions landed on a store details page.
Came for
INFERREDA ₹20K to ₹80K piece they want to be sure of: real stones, the right size, a way back if it disappoints. The bag works as a shortlist, and many finish in a store.
Bag gives today
OBSERVEDPrice, weight, a BIS badge. No certificate line, no store option, no EMI, no way to save or share the bag.
Should give
"Available at 3 Mia stores near 560045, reserve to try", the certificate in the box, returns in one line, EMI on the stone value.

2. The festival coin buyer

4,745coins added (3,126 gold, 1,619 silver); 53% of gold coins reach checkout
Who
OBSERVEDThe bag's best converters: 12.6% of gold coins added are bought. 56% of units bought in homepage-landing sessions are coins (92 of 165). 57,249 sessions land on "Gold rate today"; 3 buy.
Came for
INFERREDA known weight at today's rate, delivered before Dhanteras, with purity they can trust.
Bag gives today
OBSERVEDName, price, weight. No rate used, no purity, no "delivered by" until a pincode is typed.
Should give
"24 kt, 999.9 purity, priced at ₹X/g today, delivered by 6 Nov". On the gold rate page, a coin you can buy at that rate.

3. The silver and small-ticket buyer

10,727silver pieces added; 19% reach checkout, 5.1% bought
Who
OBSERVEDAverage unit value ₹1,800 to ₹3,400 (toe rings ₹1,824, anklets ₹3,102). Site searches: "Silver ring" 986, "Payal" 398, "Anklet" 355.
Came for
INFERREDA quick, low-risk purchase. At ₹1,800, ₹500 off is a quarter of the price, so MIA500 decides the sale.
Bag gives today
OBSERVEDA login wall before checkout, and MIA500 refused for 3,477 people.
Should give
The fastest path in the store: guest checkout, the code applied for those who qualify, free delivery said once.

4. The gifter

60%of visitors Google can identify are men
Who
OBSERVED"Under ₹15K" is the most clicked price tile (2,082 users). 3,333 people had a birthday or anniversary code refused; 158 had one accepted. INFERRED Many are buying for someone else's birthday, not their own.
Came for
INFERREDA present that arrives on time, looks like a present, and can be exchanged by the person who receives it.
Bag gives today
OBSERVEDNo gift message, no wrap, no gift receipt in the bag.
Should give
"Add a gift message", free wrap, delivery date, and a line on exchange for the recipient.

5. The returning member

16%of orders redeem loyalty points (182 of 1,118)
Who
OBSERVED4,608 people opened the bag already logged in. Returning desktop visitors who land on the homepage convert at 1.18%, the best segment on the site.
Came for
INFERREDTo buy again quickly and use what they have earned.
Checkout gives today
OBSERVED (team walkthrough) A new address is not selected after it is added; billing is asked separately; points sit in a block they must open.
Should give
Saved address preselected, points balance shown, one tap to pay.
